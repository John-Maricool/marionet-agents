# Legal review basics

## Ground rules
- Output is a draft or issue list, **not legal advice**. Say so, and mark what a qualified lawyer in the governing jurisdiction must confirm.
- Never invent statutes, section numbers, case names, fees or deadlines. Cite official sources or mark as unverified.
- Always confirm governing law and jurisdiction first.

## Clause checklist (service agreements)
1. **Parties:** correct legal names and registration numbers.
2. **Scope:** deliverables defined; change requests priced separately.
3. **Payment:** amounts, currency, milestones, due dates, late fees, what happens if unpaid (pause work, retain IP until paid).
4. **IP:** client owns project deliverables after full payment; the supplier keeps pre-existing tools, libraries and know-how, licensed to the client.
5. **Liability:** capped (commonly at fees paid under the agreement); no unlimited indemnities; exclusion of indirect loss.
6. **Warranties:** limited period for bug fixes; no promise of uninterrupted service.
7. **Confidentiality:** mutual, with standard exceptions.
8. **Termination:** for convenience with notice; payment for work done.
9. **Data protection:** who is controller and processor; a DPA if personal data is processed.
10. **Disputes:** governing law, venue, negotiation or mediation first.
11. **Third-party costs:** hosting, app store fees, API costs paid by whom.

## Risk rating
- High: unlimited liability, IP grab of pre-existing code, payment only on "client satisfaction", penalty clauses.
- Medium: vague scope, long warranty, one-sided termination.
- Low: wording and formatting.
