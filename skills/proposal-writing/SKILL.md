# Proposal writing

## Structure
1. **Their need** in their words, in the first two lines.
2. **Outcome** they will get (not the tech first).
3. **Approach:** how we will do it, briefly.
4. **Relevant proof:** only real, permitted examples.
5. **Scope and milestones:** what is included and not included.
6. **Timeline** as ranges with assumptions.
7. **Price:** approved rates or [RATE]; payment milestones.
8. **Next step:** one clear call to action (a call, a reply).

## Rules
- Short: busy clients skim. Plain English.
- Never invent rates, clients, results or team size.
- Name clients only with permission.
- Keep company work separate from a founder's previous employers unless approved.
- For freelance platforms: answer the client's questions directly and reference a specific detail from their post.
