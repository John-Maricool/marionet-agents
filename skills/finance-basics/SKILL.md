# Finance basics

Not tax or accounting advice. Use a qualified accountant for tax and statutory filings.

## Rules
- Do all arithmetic in a spreadsheet or code, never in prose.
- Separate facts (bank, invoices) from assumptions (pipeline, growth).
- Founder approves and makes every payment; agents only prepare.
- Record currency on every figure; note exchange rates and their date when converting.

## Key numbers
- **Cash runway** = cash in bank ÷ average monthly net burn.
- **Project margin** = (price − direct costs, including staff time at cost) ÷ price.
- **Weighted pipeline** = Σ deal value × probability.

## Invoices
Invoice number, dates, client legal name, line items, currency, payment terms, bank or payment link, late payment terms if agreed in the contract.

## Retainer pricing model inputs
Hours per month included, hourly cost, target margin, tools and hosting costs passed through, what happens to unused hours.

## Watch for
Runway under 3-6 months, invoices more than 30 days overdue, projects with negative margin, one client over about 40% of revenue.
