# Campaign planning

## 1. Objective
One measurable goal with a deadline (e.g. 20 qualified leads in 6 weeks). Not "awareness".

## 2. Audience
Who exactly, where they spend time, what they already believe, what stops them buying.

## 3. Message
One core message from positioning; proof points that are real and approved.

## 4. Channels
Pick 1-3 channels where the audience already is. For each: format, frequency, owner.

## 5. Budget and approvals
Total budget and per-channel caps. Any spend, public post or customer email needs approval before it goes out.

## 6. Tracking
UTM links on every link, conversion events defined, a single sheet or dashboard for results.

## 7. Success metrics
Leading (clicks, replies) and lagging (leads, revenue). Decide in advance what result means continue, change or stop.

## Experiments
Hypothesis, one change at a time, minimum sample before judging, record every result.
