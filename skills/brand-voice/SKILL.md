# Brand voice

- **Company:** [NAME] — [what you do in one line]

- **Personality (3-5 words):** [e.g. confident, plain-spoken, practical]
- **We say / we avoid:** [e.g. "we build" / "synergy", "cutting-edge"]
- **Approved proof points:** [only results and clients you have permission to name]

## Universal rules
- Outcomes before technology: what the reader gets, then how.
- Plain English, short sentences, short paragraphs, active voice.
- One idea per paragraph, one call to action per piece.
- Specific beats vague: numbers, names, timeframes (only real ones).

## Claims rules
- Never invent statistics, prices, results, testimonials or client names. Use placeholders like [RESULT] or [CLIENT].
- Name a client only with confirmed permission.
- Keep company work and a founder's personal or previous-employer work separate unless approved.
- No false urgency or scarcity.
