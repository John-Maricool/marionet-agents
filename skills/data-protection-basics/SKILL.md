# Data protection basics

Not legal advice. Always check the regulator's current official guidance; rules and directives change.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Principles (shared by NDPA and GDPR)
- Collect only what the feature needs (data minimisation).
- Tell users clearly what, why, who, and how long (transparency).
- Have a lawful basis: consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest, etc.
- Keep it secure; restrict access; encrypt sensitive data.
- Delete it when no longer needed.
- Honour user rights: access, correction, deletion, objection, portability.

## Where to check (official sources)
- Nigeria: the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and guidance from the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).
- EU users: the GDPR and guidance from the relevant data protection authority.
- Sector rules: payments, lending and banking (Central Bank of Nigeria), investments and digital assets (SEC Nigeria), consumer protection (FCCPC). Confirm with a lawyer.

## High-risk data (extra care, likely extra obligations)
Identity numbers (BVN, NIN), ID documents, biometrics, health, precise location, financial data, children's data.

## Breaches
Have an incident plan; notification deadlines may apply. Escalate immediately.
